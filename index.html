Glacia Authenticator

An open-source Windows app for your two-step verification codes. Glacia keeps your keys in an encrypted local vault, works offline, and offers optional encrypted Google Drive sync across your computers.

Google sign-in identifies your account. Optional sync uses Google Drive's private application data folder to store encrypted vault backups.
